# Sho't Left! — motion direction (2.18.0)

One idea runs through every moment: **the taxi has weight, and the camera is a second driver riding along.**
Nothing here changes the rules, money, dice or positions. Fast Mode scales the timings; Reduce Motion turns all of it off
(snap camera, no roll, no shake, no streaks, no orbit) and keeps colour, sound, captions and haptics.

For each moment, 3–5 options were weighed; the chosen one is marked ✔.

| Moment | Options | Chosen and why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Camera between drivers | a) straight glide (old) · b) crane: rise and pull back mid-flight, swoop down behind the next taxi ✔ · c) cut to the board, then zoom · d) whip pan with blur | **b** — the flight shows where the next taxi is on the loop, and the arrival lands behind the driver. Spring motion, so the camera never stops dead between moves. |
| 2. Launch | a) idle shudder (old) · b) squat: tail dips, engine revs, nose lifts on launch, exhaust puff ✔ · c) wheelspin smoke | **b** — reads as weight being thrown back; the rev is timed to the squat. |
| 3. Drive | a) chase cam on a lerp (old) · b) chase cam that pulls back, lowers and looks ahead with speed; body rolls out of corners; speed streaks trail the taxi in high gear; engine pitch follows real speed ✔ · c) side tracking shot | **b** — speed is felt through the camera, the body and the sound together, and the trails follow the road's curve (real relative motion, not a screen overlay). |
| 4. Arrival | a) stop dead · b) brake dive, suspension rebound that settles, brake squeal and a little tyre dust ✔ · c) drift into the rank | **b** — the stop has weight and a clear end; the squeal lands on the dive. |
| 5. Overtake | a) label + speed lines (old) · b) the taxi swerves out to the right lane, passes, tucks back in; the passed taxi rocks in its wash ✔ · c) camera swings to the side | **b** — causal: South Africa overtakes on the right. The old label and whoosh stay, on the swerve. |
| 6. Pothole | a) CSS board shake (old, shows the canvas edge) · b) 3D camera jolt that decays + the taxi bounce ✔ · c) screen flash | **b** — shakes the world, not the page. |
| 7. Fuzzy dice | a) keyframed drop (old) · b) real damped pendulum, the face flicker slows down as the swing settles, the number punches in ✔ · c) a 3D cube roll | **b** — the swing and the face both decelerate into the result, so the eye knows when it's final. |
| 8. Turn banner | a) fade plate (old) · b) a sign that swings down on a hinge · c) the plate drives in from the left, skewed like motion blur, brakes with a squash, letters settle one by one ✔ | **c** — "Sho't left" is a driving phrase; the banner arrives the way a taxi pulls in. |
| 9. Win | a) hold on the winner (old) · b) slow orbit around the winning taxi under the confetti ✔ · c) fly-over of the whole city | **b** — the one place where continuous camera motion is the point. |

Idle board: the camera stays still when nobody is moving (a still frame is cached, which keeps budget phones cool).
